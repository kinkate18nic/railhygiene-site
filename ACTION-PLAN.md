## Authority building—safe methods only

- [ ] Publish a transparent quarterly cleanliness-data note when the dataset supports meaningful findings.
- [ ] Share the methodology and downloadable aggregate with railway passenger communities, travel journalists and public-interest data groups.
- [ ] Seek links from relevant Android app directories and Indian train-travel resources.
- [ ] Use original charts or findings as the outreach asset; do not buy links or exchange mass guest posts.
- [ ] Ask real users for Google Play reviews at an appropriate post-journey moment; never incentivise or fabricate reviews.

## Do not implement

- Cloaking or different pages for Googlebot.
- Thousands of station, route or keyword pages without unique evidence.
- Copied IRCTC, Indian Railways or aggregator text.
- Fake reviews, fabricated ratings or inflated report counts.
- Hidden text, repetitive keyword blocks or misleading “official” language.
- Expired-domain links, paid link networks or mass automated comment links.

These tactics can cause manual actions, trademark confusion and permanent loss of trust.
