Frequently Asked Questions

How do I check train hygiene?

To check the historical cleanliness of your specific coach, you can enter your PNR into the RailHygiene site or search by Train Number to see past community ratings.

Are train coach ratings anonymous?

Yes, all train coach ratings submitted through RailHygiene are completely anonymous. We do not track or share your individual identity.

What specific parts of the coach can I rate?

You can provide detailed feedback on several key hygiene parameters including the cleanliness of toilets, floors, dustbins and dustbin availability.

Is RailHygiene an official Indian Railways app?

No, RailHygiene is an independent, community-driven platform. We are not officially affiliated with Indian Railways, IRCTC, or any government entity.

How do I report an immediate hygiene issue during my journey?

Use the official Indian Railways RailMadad service or call 139 for immediate assistance. You can then record the coach condition in RailHygiene to help future passengers.

Do I need an account to use RailHygiene or view ratings?

No account is needed to view train hygiene ratings. You can search for any train on our platform freely. To submit a rating, you'll simply need to input a valid PNR to verify your journey.

Will my PNR details be visible to others when I leave a review?

Absolutely not. Your PNR is strictly used to authenticate your journey and pinpoint the exact coach you traveled in. Once your feedback is submitted, the data is entirely aggregated and anonymized.
