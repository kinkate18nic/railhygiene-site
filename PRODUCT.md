# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Indian Railways passengers, primarily using Android phones while planning or taking a journey. They need to find a train quickly, understand recent community-reported coach hygiene conditions, and continue into the RailHygiene app to view or contribute feedback.

## Product Purpose

RailHygiene provides train and coach cleanliness information from community feedback. The website makes train-rating pages discoverable through search, gives mobile visitors a useful summary, and provides a reliable handoff into the Android app. Success means passengers can identify the correct train, understand what data is available, and open or install the app without confusion.

## Brand Personality

Trustworthy, practical, community-driven. Copy should be direct and transparent about what RailHygiene can and cannot do.

## Anti-references

Avoid government-service impersonation, exaggerated claims, ad-heavy travel portals, decorative marketing that delays the task, unclear app-install prompts, and interfaces that suggest RailHygiene resolves official railway complaints.

## Design Principles

1. Put the passenger's immediate train task first.
2. Make community-data coverage and freshness explicit.
3. Treat the website-to-app handoff as a core workflow.
4. Maintain trust through plain language and independence disclosures.
5. Work reliably on inexpensive and older Android phones as well as current devices.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Maintain readable contrast in bright outdoor conditions, 44–48px minimum touch targets, semantic landmarks and form labels, visible keyboard focus, text-resize resilience, reduced-motion support, and status messages that do not depend on color alone.
