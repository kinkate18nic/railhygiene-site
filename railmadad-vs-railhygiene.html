RailMadad vs RailHygiene: which should you use?

RailMadad is the official route for railway assistance and complaints. RailHygiene is an independent community record of historical coach cleanliness. They are complementary, not competing complaint apps.

Updated 28 July 2026 · Official claims linked to primary sources

The shortest answerIf you want somebody to act during the journey, use RailMadad or call 139. If you want to check past cleanliness ratings or help a future passenger, use RailHygiene.

What is RailMadad?

RailMadad is Indian Railways’ official grievance redressal and passenger-assistance platform. Its official FAQ describes online, app, SMS and helpline routes for train or station complaints and suggestions. The Ministry of Railways has also identified 139 as the integrated railway helpline for enquiries, complaints and assistance during travel.

Use RailMadad when the desired outcome is action: a dirty toilet needs attention, a coach facility is not working, or you need another form of official help. Give accurate train, coach and journey details. The available form fields and categories can change, so follow the current instructions on the official portal.

What is RailHygiene?

RailHygiene records community observations for future passengers. A traveller can use the Android app to connect feedback to a journey and rate the overall coach, floor, toilets and dustbins. The public website aggregates available ratings by train and reported coach, shows sample size and identifies the latest recorded journey date.

RailHygiene does not represent an inspection, guarantee a future condition or forward a complaint to railway personnel. It is intended to make scattered passenger experiences easier to compare over time. Its methodology page explains small-sample warnings, data refreshes and limitations.

Can you use both?

Yes. Suppose a toilet is unusable during your journey. First, use RailMadad or 139 because you need official assistance. After the journey, you can add an accurate RailHygiene rating for that coach. The complaint seeks immediate resolution; the community rating helps the next passenger see historical evidence.

The reverse order is not useful during an active problem. Leaving a low community rating does not alert railway staff, and a historical average cannot tell you whether the coach has just been cleaned. Keep the functions separate.

Which service uses a PNR?

Official RailMadad flows may request journey details, including a PNR or other travel authority depending on the complaint route. RailHygiene uses a valid PNR in the Android app to create a journey card and associate feedback with the relevant train and coach. The public ratings website does not publish passenger PNRs. Read more in the PNR cleanliness guide and privacy policy.

What if you only want to check a train?

Use RailHygiene’s public train cleanliness search. Search by train number, name, source or destination. If reports exist, the train page separates category averages and shows the report count. If no reports exist, the absence of data is not a cleanliness verdict.
